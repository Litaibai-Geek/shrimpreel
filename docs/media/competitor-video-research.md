These examples were reviewed to understand presentation patterns: portrait input, reference performance, full generated result, and short performance close-ups.

## What to learn from them

1. Show the original portrait and generated result together; this explains the transformation immediately.
2. Put the full vertical result first, then use short close-up clips to show the singer, band, and instruments.
3. Keep the video controls unobtrusive: muted autoplay, loop, poster frame, and click-to-play sound.
4. Clearly distinguish the reference performance from the generated result.
